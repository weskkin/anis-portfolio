About Me

I'm Anis Douar — a video editor, content creator, and community manager with over 5 years of experience.

I create engaging video content that connects with audiences and drives growth. From concept to final cut, I handle every step of the production process — scripting, editing, motion graphics, and publishing — while also managing online communities and social media strategies.

My work spans two YouTube channels: Movies Consigliere (cinematic deep-dives into iconic films) and Into The Unknown (science, history, and the mysteries of our world). Together they've reached millions of viewers worldwide.

Whether you need a video editor, a community manager, or a creative partner — I'm here to help.
