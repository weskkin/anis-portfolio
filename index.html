About Me

I'm Anis Douar, the creator behind Movies Consigliere.

I specialize in crafting deep-dive video essays that dissect the narratives, characters, and hidden details of cinema's greatest works — from the crime epics of Scorsese and Coppola to the psychological thrillers of Fincher and Tarantino.

Every video is a journey beneath the surface: uncovering what really happened, why characters made the choices they did, and the storytelling techniques that make these films timeless. I combine meticulous research, compelling narration, and polished editing to transform complex film analysis into engaging, cinematic content.

Whether it's exposing the real history behind Goodfellas, decoding the visual language of The Godfather, or unraveling the mind games of Pulp Fiction — my goal is to make you see your favorite films in a completely new light.
